Privacy Policy — Rivet Rush

Last updated: 20 October 2026
Application ID: com.freitagskind.rivetrush

The short version

Rivet Rush is an offline single-player game. It collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it does not request the Internet permission or any other permission.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data, and it contains no third-party SDKs that do.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: your current level, your scrap balance, your Workshop upgrades, which tutorial tips you have seen, and your sound, music and vibration settings. This data never leaves your device through the game. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your personal device backup, which is governed by Google's privacy policy. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes the data.

Children

Rivet Rush is intended for players aged 13 and over. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
