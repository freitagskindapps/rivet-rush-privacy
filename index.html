Privacy Policy — Rivet Rush

Last updated: 21 October 2026
Application ID: com.freitagskind.rivetrush

The short version

Rivet Rush has no accounts and no analytics, and we — the developer — collect nothing about you ourselves. The game does show ads, supplied by Google AdMob, and the AdMob software inside the app collects some information in order to serve and measure them. That is the only data collection in the app, and it is described below.

Data we collect ourselves

None. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data, contacts, photos, precise location, or usage analytics, and we hold no server that your device talks to.

Advertising

The game displays ads through Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad between runs (after every fourth finished run, on the way out of the results screen), and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch after a run to double the scrap you earned. Ads never appear during a run.

To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate, city-level location can be inferred), device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's privacy policy for partner sites and apps. We never receive it in a form that identifies you.

There are no in-app purchases, so no payment information is ever handled.

Your choices

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom the game asks for your consent before requesting its first ad, and an Ad privacy button on the main menu reopens that choice at any time so you can change or withdraw it.

Everywhere, Android lets you reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, from Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

Data the game stores on your device

Your progress — current level, scrap balance, Workshop upgrades, which tutorial tips you have seen, how many runs you have finished (used to pace the ads), and your sound, music and vibration settings — is saved locally on your device only. It is never uploaded. If Android Backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this save data in your own device backup under your Google account; that backup is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours, and we have no access to it.

Uninstalling the app deletes the save data. There is nothing for us to delete on our side, because we hold nothing.

Permissions

The app declares INTERNET (the ad SDK needs it to fetch ads) and the advertising-ID permission used by Google AdMob. It requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions. The game itself — everything other than the ads — runs entirely offline.

Children

The game is directed at users aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. It is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything further that collects data — analytics, cloud saves, or leaderboards — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
